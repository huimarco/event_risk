      Stress sheet

      Adds a sheet with the question's factors and how much each moves if it resolves YES or NO now. Type your holdings' loadings (P&L per 1% of a % factor, per 1 bp of a bp factor) in the yellow column: P&L is computed in the workbook, and holdings never leave it.

      Exposure sheet

      Adds a sheet with the issuer's event exposures — every live question it is exposed to, sorted by risk, with score, signed impact and direction.

      Functions

      Questions
      =ER.QUESTIONS([query], [limit], [sort], [entity], [factor], [industry])
      Search questions. Filter by entity name ("Iran"), factor id ("brent"), or GICS industry code ("453010"). First column is the id other functions take.
      =ER.ENTITY(name, [limit])
      Questions involving a country, person or organisation, e.g. "Iran".
      =ER.PROB(id)
      Probability, 0–1.
      =ER.CHANGE(id)
      24h change in probability.
      =ER.NAME(id)
      The question asked.
      =ER.FIELD(id, field)
      One detail: volume_24h, open_interest, updated, status, headline, close_time; for a contract (mkt:) also title, rules, resolution_sources.
      =ER.RUNGS(question_id)
      A question's ladder: every deadline / threshold, with rung ids.
      =ER.CONTRACTS(id)
      The Kalshi / Polymarket contracts behind it, with their rules.
      =ER.HISTORY(id, [start], [end], [interval])
      Probability over time (daily by default).
      =ER.CURVE(question_id)
      Term structure: probability per period, conditional hazard and annualized hazard rate.
      =ER.PROB_BY(question_id, date, [extrapolate])
      P(event by a date), e.g. a bond's maturity.
      Factors
      =ER.FACTORS(id)
      Factors the question moves, with the measured move per +1.0 probability and if it resolves YES / NO.
      =ER.FACTOR(factor_id, [field])
      A factor's latest level (default), or name, layer (macro|niche), family, units, series, definition.
      =ER.FACTOR_HISTORY(factor_id, [start], [end])
      Daily levels of a factor (date, level in % or bp). Default: last year.
      =ER.SENSITIVITY(id, factor_id)
      Factor move per +1.0 of probability, in % or bp.
      =ER.STRESS(id, factor_ids, loadings, ["yes"|"no"])
      Σ loading × factor move if it resolves: your P&L, computed in Excel.
      =ER.FACTOR_SECURITIES(factor_id, [limit])
      Securities with a measured loading on a factor, largest |t| first.
      Issuers & Securities
      =ER.INDUSTRIES([query], [sector])
      GICS industry table with 6-digit codes. Use a code in ER.QUESTIONS industry filter.
      =ER.ISSUERS([query], [limit])
      Search issuers by name. First column is the id for ER.EXPOSURES.
      =ER.EXPOSURES(issuer_id, [limit], [sort])
      Questions the issuer is exposed to: prob, score, signed impact, direction and risk. Sort: risk (default), score, impact.
      =ER.SECURITIES(question_id, [limit])
      Securities with a measured beta to a question: ticker, beta, move if YES/NO.
      =ER.LOADINGS(ticker)
      A security's factor loadings: beta, se, t-stat. |t| ≥ 3 rows first.
      =ER.SECURITY_EXPOSURES(ticker, [limit], [sort])
      Questions a security moves with: beta, move if YES/NO, risk, found_by. Sort: risk, move, evidence.

      Ids: q:… question  ·  prop:… one rung  ·  mkt:… one venue contract  ·  iss:… issuer.
      A question's probability is its headline rung: the live rung with the most trading in the last 24h. Use a rung id from RUNGS to track one deadline over time.
